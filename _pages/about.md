Hi, I am **Zhengling Yangli (杨李正凌)**, a Statistics undergraduate at [Yunnan University](https://www.ynu.edu.cn/), enrolled in 2024. My work brings together statistics, combinatorial optimization, and computer science. I enjoy taking a problem from a mathematical formulation to an implementation, then testing what the results actually tell us.

Mathematics and programming competitions helped me connect coursework with concrete problems. Working on MathRAG, a mathematical question-answering system, led me to think more about implementation and reliable reasoning. My subsequent optimization projects taught me to ask whether an improved metric reflects a meaningful improvement in solving the problem. These experiences shape my attention to explicit assumptions, reproducible experiments, and careful interpretation.

#### Selected research

- **NLIPSat · SAT 2026, published; first author.** A toolkit for encoding bounded polynomial nonlinear integer programs into MaxSAT, developed with collaborators at Renmin University of China and Université de Picardie Jules Verne. I attended FloC / SAT 2026 in Lisbon to present and discuss the work. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.SAT.2026.43) · [Code](https://github.com/ZhenglingYangli/NLIPSat-Toolkit)
- **Instance-specific reduction policies for Minimum Hitting Set · accepted at PRICAI 2026.** The paper is titled _Learning Instance-Specific Reduction Policies for the Minimum Hitting Set Problem_. Proceedings details will be added when available. [Publications and accepted work]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})
- **Ongoing exploration.** My projects include search and reduction in combinatorial optimization, fair multi-resource allocation, and retrieval-augmented mathematical question answering. These experiences help me identify the questions I want to pursue further. [Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }})
