NLIPSat

How can a logic solver tackle nonlinear integer programs?

A toolkit that encodes bounded polynomial nonlinear integer programs into MaxSAT, connecting mathematical modeling, logical representation, and solver implementation.

Developed with collaborators at Renmin University of China and Université de Picardie Jules Verne. Presented and discussed at FloC / SAT 2026 in Lisbon.
